Indicators

  • ATR (14) — How big is a normal candle move? TIP’s volatility yardstick.
  • Donchian (20) — Highest high and lowest low over a lookback — the breakout ceiling/floor.
  • Volume SMA (20) — Average traded volume — the baseline for a “busy day” check.

Patterns

  • Breakout — Close above the prior 20-bar high, then track confirmation, retest, and targets.
  • Breakdown — Close below the prior 20-bar low — the short mirror of breakout.
  • Consolidation — A multi-bar tight range vs ATR — a volatility regime, not a buy/sell call at detect.
  • Volume breakout — Unusually large volume plus a wide bar — even without a Donchian break.
  • Hammer — Long lower wick, small body — potential bullish pin after pressure lower.
  • Shooting star — Long upper wick, small body — potential bearish pin after a push higher.
  • Bullish engulfing — A green body that fully covers the prior red body — two-bar bullish reversal shape.
  • Bearish engulfing — A red body that fully covers the prior green body — two-bar bearish reversal shape.
  • Inside bar — A bar fully inside the mother bar, then a close that breaks the mother range.
  • Higher high — A confirmed swing high that is higher than the previous swing high.
  • Lower low — A confirmed swing low that is lower than the previous swing low.
