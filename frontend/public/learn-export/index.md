## Patterns

- [Breakout](./patterns/breakout.md) — Close above the prior 20-bar high, then track confirmation, retest, and targets. *(SPA: /learn/patterns/breakout)*
- [Breakdown](./patterns/breakdown.md) — Close below the prior 20-bar low — the short mirror of breakout. *(SPA: /learn/patterns/breakdown)*
- [Consolidation](./patterns/consolidation.md) — A multi-bar tight range vs ATR — a volatility regime, not a buy/sell call at detect. *(SPA: /learn/patterns/consolidation)*
- [Volume breakout](./patterns/volume_breakout.md) — Unusually large volume plus a wide bar — even without a Donchian break. *(SPA: /learn/patterns/volume_breakout)*
- [Hammer](./patterns/hammer.md) — Long lower wick, small body — potential bullish pin after pressure lower. *(SPA: /learn/patterns/hammer)*
- [Shooting star](./patterns/shooting_star.md) — Long upper wick, small body — potential bearish pin after a push higher. *(SPA: /learn/patterns/shooting_star)*
- [Bullish engulfing](./patterns/bullish_engulfing.md) — A green body that fully covers the prior red body — two-bar bullish reversal shape. *(SPA: /learn/patterns/bullish_engulfing)*
- [Bearish engulfing](./patterns/bearish_engulfing.md) — A red body that fully covers the prior green body — two-bar bearish reversal shape. *(SPA: /learn/patterns/bearish_engulfing)*
- [Inside bar](./patterns/inside_bar.md) — A bar fully inside the mother bar, then a close that breaks the mother range. *(SPA: /learn/patterns/inside_bar)*
- [Higher high](./patterns/higher_high.md) — A confirmed swing high that is higher than the previous swing high. *(SPA: /learn/patterns/higher_high)*
- [Lower low](./patterns/lower_low.md) — A confirmed swing low that is lower than the previous swing low. *(SPA: /learn/patterns/lower_low)*
- [Double bottom](./patterns/double_bottom.md) — Two similar troughs; long when a closed bar breaks the intervening peak. *(SPA: /learn/patterns/double_bottom)*
- [Double top](./patterns/double_top.md) — Two similar peaks; short when a closed bar breaks the intervening trough. *(SPA: /learn/patterns/double_top)*
- [Head and shoulders](./patterns/head_shoulders.md) — Shoulder–head–shoulder; short on a close through the (possibly sloped) neckline. *(SPA: /learn/patterns/head_shoulders)*
- [Inverse head and shoulders](./patterns/inverse_head_shoulders.md) — Inverted H&S; long on a close through the neckline joining the two armpit highs. *(SPA: /learn/patterns/inverse_head_shoulders)*
